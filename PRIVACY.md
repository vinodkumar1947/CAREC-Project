# Privacy and Data Rules

CAREC currently requires no cloud service and must not collect participant data
for normal development. Contributors may use synthetic scenarios and
non-identifiable test fixtures only.

Do not commit faces, voices, locations, wheelchair identifiers, health details,
caregiver contact details, wireless credentials or raw data from a person
without a separately approved protocol and storage plan. Telemetry must be
data-minimized, documented, time-bounded and disabled by default when it is not
needed.

Any future human study requires owner approval plus appropriate consent,
privacy, retention, access, deletion and ethics/regulatory review before data
collection begins.
