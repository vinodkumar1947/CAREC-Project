# AI/ML Workstream

Status: Proposed
Owner: AI/ML Workstream
Last Updated: 2026-08-23
Related Issues: CARE-023, CARE-024
Related ADRs: ADR-0007

Owns model evaluation, behavior prediction, semantic assistance, experiment tracking, and inference packaging. Favor small reproducible baselines and CPU-capable workflows. Training data must be licensed, privacy-reviewed, versioned, and representative. AI suggestions remain subordinate to deterministic safety constraints.
