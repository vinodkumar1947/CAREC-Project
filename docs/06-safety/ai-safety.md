# AI Safety

Status: Preliminary
Owner: AI/ML and Safety Workstreams
Last Updated: 2026-08-23
Related Issues: CARE-024, CARE-039
Related ADRs: ADR-0007

Models must document intended use, excluded conditions, data provenance, performance slices, latency, calibration, uncertainty, known failure modes, and monitoring. Test lighting, occlusion, mobility aids, varied body characteristics, clutter, reflective surfaces, and domain shift. AI output may not bypass speed limits, watchdogs, emergency stop, or deterministic obstacle constraints.
