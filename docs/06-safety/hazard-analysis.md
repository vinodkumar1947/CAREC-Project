# Hazard Analysis Method

Status: Preliminary
Owner: Safety Workstream
Last Updated: 2026-08-23
Related Issues: CARE-035, CARE-036
Related ADRs: TBD

For each foreseeable sequence record the initiating fault or use condition, hazardous situation, potential harm, affected users, initial severity/probability, controls, verification, residual risk, owner, and status. Analyze emergency stop, communication timeout, sensor and compute failure, battery and motor-controller failure, obstacle-detection failure, AI misclassification, localization/navigation loss, acceleration, collision, tip-over, stairs/drop-offs, manual override, and caregiver intervention.

Risk scales are TBD and require maintainer approval before numerical ranking. Unknown probability must remain unknown rather than being guessed.
