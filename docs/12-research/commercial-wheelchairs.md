# Commercial Wheelchairs

Status: Draft
Owner: Hardware and Research Workstreams
Last Updated: 2026-08-23
Related Issues: CARE-003, CARE-029
Related ADRs: ADR-0004

Research commercial systems only from current manufacturer or authoritative documentation. Record drive configuration, approved interfaces, braking and stop behavior, dimensions, payload, electrical limits, service constraints, accessibility controls, and warranty/regulatory boundaries. Do not infer compatibility from connector appearance or generic protocol claims. No CAREC universal adapter is assumed.
