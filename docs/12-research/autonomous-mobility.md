# Autonomous Mobility Research

Status: Draft
Owner: Research Workstream
Last Updated: 2026-08-23
Related Issues: CARE-016 through CARE-024
Related ADRs: ADR-0006, ADR-0007

Track indoor service robots, autonomous vehicles, assistive navigation, shared-control wheelchairs, semantic mapping, dynamic-agent prediction, and safety cases. Extract transferable patterns cautiously: wheelchair users have different agency, comfort, accessibility, proximity, stopping, and human-interaction requirements. Recommendations must identify evidence gaps and simulation experiments.
