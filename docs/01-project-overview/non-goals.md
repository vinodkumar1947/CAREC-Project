# Non-Goals

Status: Proposed
Owner: Project Maintainer
Last Updated: 2026-08-23
Related Issues: TBD
Related ADRs: TBD

CAREC does not currently aim to provide road driving, outdoor unsupervised autonomy, a universal wheelchair motor adapter, diagnosis or clinical advice, remote control over the public internet, or a certified medical device. Simulation success is not evidence of physical or human safety. Cloud connectivity and AI-generated motion decisions are optional and may not bypass local safety controls.
