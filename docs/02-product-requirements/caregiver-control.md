# Caregiver Control

Status: Proposed
Owner: Mobile and Safety Workstreams
Last Updated: 2026-08-23
Related Issues: TBD
Related ADRs: ADR-0008

Caregiver functions may include authorized stop, mode/status visibility, and destination assistance. Authentication, consent, auditability, availability, privacy, and loss-of-connectivity behavior are required before implementation. Remote caregiver commands shall not bypass local user controls or the independent safety supervisor. Internet-dependent motion control is out of scope unless separately approved.
