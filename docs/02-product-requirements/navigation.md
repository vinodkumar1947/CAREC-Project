# Navigation Requirements

Status: Proposed
Owner: Navigation Workstream
Last Updated: 2026-08-23
Related Issues: TBD
Related ADRs: ADR-0006

The system shall support map creation, localization confidence, accessible goal selection, footprint-aware planning, dynamic obstacle response, cancellation, recovery, and a supervised fallback. Route success alone is insufficient: collision count, minimum clearance, stop latency, comfort limits, intervention count, and localization quality must also be captured.
