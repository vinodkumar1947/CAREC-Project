# CAREC Caregiver Guide

**You don't need to know anything about technology to use this guide.**

CAREC is a safety device attached to your child's wheelchair. It beeps and flashes colours to warn you (and the child) when there is something in the way.

---

## What CAREC Does

- Watches the area in front of the wheelchair through a small camera
- Beeps and flashes when something is too close
- Sends alerts to your phone (via the SenseCraft Mate app)
- **Does NOT stop the wheelchair automatically** — it is a warning, not a brake

---

## The Three Colours

| Display Colour | What It Means | What To Do |
|----------------|--------------|------------|
| **GREEN** (solid) | All clear — nothing close | Normal — continue |
| **YELLOW** (blinking slowly) | Something 60–100 cm ahead | Caution — slow down, look ahead |
| **RED** (blinking fast) | Something less than 60 cm ahead — very close | Stop the wheelchair now |

---

## The Beeps

| Beep Sound | What It Means |
|------------|---------------|
| One slow beep (repeating) | Yellow zone — caution |
| Three fast beeps (repeating) | Red zone — stop |
| Silence | Green zone — all clear |

---

## Before Each Use (5 Things to Check)

1. **Green light on?** — Power on and wait 10 seconds. Display should be green.
2. **Battery charged?** — The 10,000 mAh battery should have ≥ 1 LED lit.
3. **Camera clear?** — Look at the clear front of the Watcher. No smudges, tape, or scratches.
4. **Clamp tight?** — Gently shake the Watcher. It should not move.
5. **Cable safe?** — USB cable routed away from wheels.

**Quick test:** Hold your hand 30 cm in front of the camera. The display should turn RED and beep three fast times within 1 second.

---

## Charging the Battery

1. Use the USB-C cable that came with the 10,000 mAh battery
2. Plug into any standard USB-A charger (5V/2A or higher)
3. Full charge takes ~6 hours
4. CAREC runs for **50+ hours** on a full charge

---

## The SenseCraft Mate App (Optional)

The app shows the same alerts on your phone and keeps a history.

- **Download:** Search "SenseCraft Mate" on App Store or Google Play (free)
- **Pair:** Open app → tap "Add Device" → follow Bluetooth pairing steps
- **Alerts:** When CAREC sees an obstacle, a notification appears on your phone

The app is optional — CAREC beeps and flashes even without the app.

---

## What CAREC Does NOT Do

- **Does not stop the wheelchair** — always supervise
- **Does not detect obstacles behind the wheelchair** — forward-facing only
- **Does not work in the dark** — needs normal indoor lighting
- **Does not replace your supervision** — CAREC is a helper, not a babysitter

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

| Problem | Try This |
|---------|----------|
| Display is dark / no power | Check USB-C cable; check battery charge |
| Constant red alarm with nothing there | Power off (hold button 3 sec), wait 10 sec, power on |
| No beep when hand placed at 30 cm | Check battery; check if display shows green (might be muted) |
| App says "disconnected" | Still works! App is optional. Move phone closer to wheelchair. |
| Watcher is loose / fell off | Stop. Reattach clamp before resuming. |

**Emergency:** Hold the side button for 3 seconds to power off.

---

## Removing the Device (2 Minutes)

1. Unplug the USB-C cable from the Watcher
2. Loosen the clamp screw on the armrest (1–2 turns)
3. Lift the entire Watcher off the armrest
4. The wheelchair is unchanged — no tools needed, no damage
