# Acceptance Testing

Status: Proposed
Owner: Product and Safety Workstreams
Last Updated: 2026-08-23
Related Issues: TBD
Related ADRs: TBD

Acceptance criteria are observable statements attached to a milestone or issue. M0 acceptance is limited to reproducible simulation. Controlled wheelchair and supervised-user acceptance require new approved protocols, accessibility and human-factors input, risk review, consent/privacy controls where applicable, and explicit owner authorization.
