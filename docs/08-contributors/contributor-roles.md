Last Updated: 2026-08-23

Roles are Project Maintainer, Workstream Lead, Contributor, Reviewer, and Safety Reviewer. Responsibilities and decision authority are defined in [GOVERNANCE.md](../../GOVERNANCE.md). Roles are earned through sustained, respectful, reviewable contributions and may be scoped by workstream. Physical integration authority is not granted by repository role.
