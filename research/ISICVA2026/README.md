# ISICVA 2026 Research Archive

> **Historical draft, not an active submission claim.** The recorded June 15, 2026 submission deadline has passed. The repository contains no verified submission or acceptance evidence. Conference, publication, indexing, fee, and deadline information must be independently rechecked before reuse.

## What this directory contains

- an early paper outline;
- abstract and draft text;
- a preliminary bibliography;
- an old submission checklist; and
- placeholders for figures, results, and reviews.

The material predates the August 2026 simulation-first reset and focuses on the SenseCAP obstacle-warning prototype.

## Claims that require evidence

Draft statements about novelty, cost, accuracy, latency, false-positive rate, battery life, BLE behavior, optical flow, mounting, production readiness, or pediatric deployment must not be repeated as established findings.

In particular:

- the firmware has no active optical-flow motion gate;
- monocular distance estimates are heuristic;
- physical obstacle and battery validation is incomplete;
- no universal wheelchair compatibility has been demonstrated; and
- CAREC is not approved for unsupervised or occupied-wheelchair autonomy.

## Reuse path

Before reviving this paper or targeting another venue:

1. select a current research question;
2. verify the venue through authoritative sources;
3. separate legacy firmware results from CAREC Sim results;
4. preregister metrics and scenario acceptance criteria;
5. collect reproducible evidence from versioned code;
6. document limitations and negative results;
7. obtain appropriate ethics, consent, privacy, and professional review before any human-participant work; and
8. update every date, affiliation, reference, and product comparison.
