# CAREC Code of Conduct

CAREC is an accessibility and safety project. Participants must be respectful,
patient and evidence-driven; use inclusive language; protect privacy; and give
wheelchair users meaningful authority in discussions about their needs.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, unsafe deployment encouragement,
fabricated evidence, and publication of private or sensitive information are
not accepted. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through
the verified GitHub profile. The owner may edit, reject, lock or remove content
and restrict participation when necessary to protect people or the project.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. Critique the design and evidence,
not the contributor.
